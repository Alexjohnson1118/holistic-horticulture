Garden-Ready Fitness

Gardening is a full-body job — digging, kneeling, lifting, reaching overhead for hours at a time. Emelye is a European & UK CrossFit Champion, qualified personal trainer and former sponsored athlete, and brings that training into the garden: simple, practical sessions to help you move better, lift safely, and keep going without your back giving out halfway through a border.
