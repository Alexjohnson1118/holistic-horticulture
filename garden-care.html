Our approach

Healthy soil, left well alone

Every visit follows the same organic principles — no shortcuts, no chemicals, no exceptions.

No-dig beds

We build soil structure from the top down with mulch and compost, rather than disturbing it every season.

Peat-free, always

Every compost and mulch we use is peat-free — better for your soil, and for the bogs it didn't come from.

Pesticide-free pest control

Companion planting, barriers, and patience — not a sprayer. If something's eating your plants, we work out why first.
