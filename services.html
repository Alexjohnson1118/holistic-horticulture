Garden services

Care, design, learning — and the strength to do it yourself

Every service follows the same holistic approach: organic methods, healthy soil first, and no synthetic chemicals, ever.

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦
01 — Ongoing care

Organic Garden Maintenance

Regular, considered upkeep that works with your garden's natural rhythms rather than against them. No synthetic pesticides, herbicides or fertilisers — ever.

No-dig beds Peat-free Pesticide-free pest control Seasonal pruning & feeding

Includes: weeding, pruning, deadheading, mulching, composting guidance, lawn care (organic), and seasonal planting. From £32/hour — in line with the going rate for qualified, organic-specialist gardening around Portsmouth.

02 — New & existing gardens

Garden Design & Planning

Just moved and facing a blank, overgrown, or awkward plot? Or ready to finally revamp the garden you've outgrown? We'll plan it with you from first sketch to final planting.

New-build & house-move gardens Full garden revamps Planting plans Phased & budget-friendly planning

Includes: site assessment (soil, light, drainage), layout & planting plans, plant sourcing advice, and phased project planning if you'd rather spread the work over a few seasons. Initial consultation is free; full planting & layout plans from £350.

✦ ✦ ✦ ✦
03 — Learn as you go

The Potting Shed Sessions

Our hands-on, in-your-own-garden workshops. Rather than one-off advice, Emelye teaches you to understand and care for your specific space — and makes a genuinely lovely gift for the gardener (or reluctant gardener) in your life.

See workshop formats & pricing →
04 — Stay strong for it

Garden-Ready Fitness

Gardening is a full-body job — digging, kneeling, lifting, reaching overhead for hours at a time. Emelye is a European & UK CrossFit Champion, qualified personal trainer and former sponsored athlete, and brings that training into the garden: simple, practical sessions to help you move better, lift safely, and keep going without your back giving out halfway through a border.

Mobility & strength for digging & lifting 1:1 personal training Can be combined with a Potting Shed Session

From £40/session — in line with Portsmouth-area personal training rates, from someone who's actually competed at the top of her sport.
