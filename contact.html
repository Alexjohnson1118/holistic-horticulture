Good to know

Booking, travel & weather

Local gardens

Up to 20 minutes from Alverstoke — no travel fee. Minimum booking of 2 hours.

Further afield

Call-out fees apply: £20 (20–40 min), £30 (40–60 min), £40 (60+ min) each way. A half- or full-day minimum booking may be required. Beyond this, travel is discussed case by case.

Weather-dependent work

Light rain won't stop a visit — proper wet-weather gear is standard kit. Severe weather or saturated ground may mean rescheduling certain tasks for safety.
