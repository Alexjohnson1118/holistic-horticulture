Garden Design & Planning

Just moved and facing a blank, overgrown, or downright confusing plot? Or ready to finally revamp the garden you've outgrown? We'll plan it with you — in person, on your actual soil — from first sketch to final planting.

How it works

Planned properly, in four steps

01

Free consultation

Emelye walks the whole plot with you — soil, light, drainage, what's already there, and what you actually want from the space.

02

Concept & planting plan

A layout and planting plan built for your specific garden — not a generic template, and definitely not guesswork.

03

Phased roadmap

A realistic, budget-friendly order of works — so you can do it all at once, or spread it over a few seasons.

04

Planting & follow-up

We can plant it ourselves, hand it to your preferred contractor, or you can do it yourself with a plan to follow.

A note, tongue only partly in cheek: we won't be asking AI to design your garden. It's remarkable at a great many things — none of them involve knowing how big your shed actually is, where that drain cover sits, or that your "full sun" border is shaded solid by 2pm. Proper planning starts with a specialist, standing in your actual garden.
