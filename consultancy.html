A complete, strategic picture of your garden — mapped, planned, and calendared. What you get

Real investigation. Real solutions.

Not generic tips — proper problem-solving. Emelye gets to the actual root cause, then tells you exactly how to fix it.

Thorough on-site investigation

A proper walk of the whole garden with you — no rushing, no generic checklist.

Root-cause diagnosis

Plant ID, pest and disease problems, soil issues, why that corner never thrives — properly investigated, not guessed at.

Garden mapping

A proper site map of your garden — layout, zones, planting areas — the kind of reference you'll use for years.

Seasonal calendar

A month-by-month plan of what to do and when — pruning windows, feeding, sowing, the lot.

Written action plan

A prioritised report tying it all together — what matters most, in what order.

Plant selection & sourcing

Specific recommendations on what to plant and where to get it — including native plant guidance.
