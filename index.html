Meet Emelye

RHS Level 2 trained, European & UK CrossFit Champion

Emelye Dwyer founded Holistic Horticulture to offer a gentler, more sustainable alternative to conventional garden maintenance — built on organic methods, patient observation, and sharing knowledge rather than gatekeeping it. She also brings a competitive athlete's eye to the physical side of gardening: how to lift, dig and move without wrecking your body.
